Supplier Empanelment

A structured three-phase onboarding process.

Supplier evaluation is designed around company capability, product quality and international trade readiness.

Phase 01 01

Company & Eligibility

We collect the core information required to understand your business structure, operating location, years of experience and ability to supply.

  • • Legal / trading name
  • • Business location and operating hubs
  • • Company type and years in operation
  • • Export experience
  • • Certifications and registrations
Phase 02 02

Product & Quality

We assess the product categories, specifications, capacity, quality controls and documentation available for potential international procurement.

  • • Product categories
  • • Product specifications
  • • Production / supply capacity
  • • Quality control systems
  • • Product certificates and test reports
Phase 03 03

Trade Readiness

We establish whether the supplier can support commercial terms, packaging, logistics, documentation and international shipment requirements.

  • • Incoterms and payment preferences
  • • MOQ and lead times
  • • Export documentation
  • • Packaging and loading capability
  • • International logistics readiness
