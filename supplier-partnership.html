Empanelment Journey

A clear three-phase review.

We normally aim to review a complete application within 1–3 business days. Additional verification may be requested where a product, licence or destination requires it.

01

Company & Eligibility

Business identity, ownership/contact information, registrations, facility details, years in operation, manufacturing or trading role, export capability and current markets.

02

Product & Quality

Products, grades, specifications, origin, capacity, MOQ, packaging, certifications, photographs, catalogs, samples, testing and quality-control capability.

03

Trade Readiness

Incoterms, payment terms, nearest port/ICD, lead times, documentation, export history, private-label/OEM capability, cold-chain capability where relevant and commercial quotation readiness.

Information submitted is intended for Adhil International’s supplier evaluation and sourcing process.
